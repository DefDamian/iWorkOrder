This Privacy Policy applies exclusively to users of the iWorkOrder by DCA mobile application located within the United States. By using the app, you confirm that you are a U.S.-based user.
§ 01

Scope

iWorkOrder by DCA ("we", "our", or "us") — developed by Damian Cedeno Almeida (DCA) — is a mobile application designed for property maintenance workflow management, intended exclusively for use within the United States. By downloading and using the app, you confirm that you are located in the U.S. and agree to this Privacy Policy.

§ 02

Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide
  • Property details (addresses, building names)
  • Work orders, notes, and descriptions
  • Photos and attachments (if used)
  • User-entered names (e.g., superintendent, handyman)
B. Automatically Stored Data
  • Timestamps of actions
  • Audit logs (immutable records of changes)
  • Device identifiers (limited, non-tracking)
C. iCloud Storage
☁️
All data is stored in your Apple iCloud account

We do not store your data on any external or third-party servers. All user data is stored using Apple iCloud services, under your personal Apple ID. Apple may process your data under their own privacy policies.

apple.com/legal/privacy/ ↗

§ 05

Your Rights (U.S. Users)

Depending on your state, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

🔍 Access
Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
🗑 Deletion
Request deletion of your personal data from our systems.
✏️ Correction
Request that inaccurate or incomplete data be corrected.
📤 Export
Request your data in a portable, machine-readable format.
Because all data is stored in iCloud, most deletion and export actions can be performed directly through your device or Apple iCloud settings — giving you full control without needing to contact us.
§ 06

Data Retention

  • Data is retained as long as it exists in your iCloud account
  • Audit logs are not altered or deleted automatically — they are preserved for legal integrity
  • You control deletion directly via your Apple account and device settings
§ 07

Data Security

We rely on Apple's robust security infrastructure to protect your data:

  • Apple iCloud end-to-end encryption
  • iOS device-level security (Face ID, passcode, Secure Enclave)
  • No external databases or third-party servers
Despite these protections, no system is 100% secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, please contact Apple Support and reach out to us immediately.
§ 08

Children's Privacy

iWorkOrder by DCA is a professional tool intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from individuals under 18. If you believe a minor has used our app, please contact us immediately.

§ 09

Third-Party Services

iWorkOrder integrates with exactly one third-party service:

☁️
Apple iCloud — the only third party we rely on

We use no analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, and no third-party trackers of any kind. Your data stays between you and Apple.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. When we make significant changes, we will notify you within the app. Continued use of iWorkOrder by DCA after any update constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
